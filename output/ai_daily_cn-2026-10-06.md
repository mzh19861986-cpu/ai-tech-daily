This is noteworthy because neutrinos barely interact with matter and can carry information from the universe's most extreme objects (such as supernovae and black holes) straight through space to Earth, and Halzen turned the entire Antarctic ice sheet into a telescope, opening a completely new window for humanity to observe the universe.
